# 6. UI/UX Guidelines

## Brand
- Deep purple primary colour
- Teal accent
- White/light neutral backgrounds
- Modern sans-serif typography
- Clear icons and authentic service photography

Brand copy:
- SkyTouchClean
- Clean Spaces. Fresh Clothes. Less Worries.
- WE CLEAN. WE WASH. WE FUMIGATE.
- SKYTOUCHCLEAN — HONESTY AND SERVICE.

## Navigation
Home | Services | Pricing | About | Contact | Book Now. Keep call/WhatsApp action visible on mobile.

## Home page
1. Header and primary booking action
2. Hero statement and service summary
3. Cleaning, Laundry and Fumigation cards
4. Approved prices/starting prices
5. Free laundry pickup and delivery highlight
6. How it works
7. Trust/service-quality section
8. Contact, address and footer

## Pricing display
- Home cleaning: compact table/cards with monthly and individual visit columns.
- State clearly that monthly packages include four visits.
- Laundry: item-based prices; do not show native wear per kg.
- Duvets: “From ₦3,500”.
- Fumigation: “Request a quotation”.
- Explain extra charges and assessment before submission.

## Forms
Keep forms short and step-based: service details → contact/address → date/time/notes → review/submit. Show estimate only where possible and explain that staff may confirm final scope/price.

## Admin
Dashboard summary, separate service queues, search/filter, clear status labels, customer details and internal notes.

## Accessibility
Mobile-first, readable font sizes, adequate contrast, labelled fields, keyboard navigation, visible focus states and useful inline validation. Do not rely on colour alone for statuses.
